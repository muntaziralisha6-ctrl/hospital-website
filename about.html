Our Journey

Healing Beyond Boundaries

Aetheris Hospital was founded in 1998 with a simple mission: bring world-class medical care to every individual — regardless of where they come from. What started as a 50-bed facility has grown into a 500+ bed multi-specialty tertiary care hospital, recognized globally for clinical excellence.

Over 25+ years, we have treated over 500,000 patients, performed groundbreaking surgeries, and pioneered minimally invasive techniques. Our commitment to research, innovation, and compassion has made us a trusted name in modern medicine.

Today, we are proud to be JCI-accredited, ranked among the top hospitals for patient safety and outcomes, and continuously pushing the boundaries of what's possible in healthcare.
