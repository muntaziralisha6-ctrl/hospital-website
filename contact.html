FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about contacting and visiting Aetheris.

How do I reach an appointment?
You can reach out through our contact page, call +1 (800) 892-4490, or use our mobile app. Same-day appointments are available for urgent cases.
What insurance plans do you accept?
We accept all major insurance providers including Aetna, Blue Cross, Cigna, United Health, and many international plans. Contact our billing team for specifics.
Do you offer telemedicine consultations?
Yes. We offer virtual consultations for follow-ups, second opinions, and non-emergency inquiries. Reach out through our website or app.
How do I get my medical records?
Request your records through our patient portal or email records@aetheris.health. Records are typically ready within 3 business days.
Is parking available at the hospital?
Yes — we have 500+ free parking spaces including dedicated accessible parking near each entrance. Valet service is available at the main entrance.
